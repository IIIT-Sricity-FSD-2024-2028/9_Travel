
# Travel Partner & Vendor Coordination Platform
# (Travel and Hospitality – Beyond Booking) - DOMAIN

## Problem Statement - Travel Partner & Vendor Coordination Platform
Most travel websites are limited to booking tickets or hotels. They do not address the coordination between travel partners, vendors, and travel guides. This results in delays, service mismatches, and a poor travel experience.

The objective of this project is to develop a Travel Partner & Vendor Coordination Platform that extends beyond booking and facilitates a smooth coordination between customers, travel partners, vendors, travel guides and support executive.

---

## Actors Identified

1. Traveler  
2. Travel Partner  
3. Vendor (Hotel / Transport)  
4. Travel Guide
5. Support Executive

---

## Planned Features for Each Actor

### Traveler
- Views itinerary 
- Confirms trip plans
- Receives updates
- Report Service issues

### Travel Partner
- Prepare Coordination plans
- Requests vendor services
- Assigns tour guides
- Receive Execution updates
- Manages coordination workflow

### Vendor
- Accepts or rejects service request
- Provides service updates
- Confirms availability

### Travel Guide
- Receives assigned tours
- Provides guided tour updates
- Supports execution of travel plan

### Support Executive
- Handles coordination issues
- Helps resolve problems during trips

---

# System Workflow

## Trip Planning Phase
- Travel partner requests vendor availability. Vendor accept or reject requests, and tour guide duties are assigned.

## Pre-Trip Preparation
- Traveler receives itinerary details and confirms the trip plan.

## Trip Execution
- Vendors and tour guides provide updates. Travel partner monitors coordination status.

##  Issue Handling
- If problems occur, support executive coordinates resolution and sends updates.

## Trip Completion
- Services are completed and final notifications are sent.
